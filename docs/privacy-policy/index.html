1. About this policy

This notice explains how Rizal Bin Jamin (“FieldWorks”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the FieldWorks Android app. It applies to users in the United States, the European Economic Area (EEA), Malaysia, and Singapore. Privacy laws differ across the United States and Asia, and this notice is supplemented by any mandatory local rights or notices that apply to you.

FieldWorks is a project journal for recording project details and moments such as photos, videos, written notes, and voice notes.

2. Information we handle

Account and sign-in information

If you create an account or sign in, Firebase Authentication processes information needed to authenticate you and manage your account. Depending on your sign-in method, this may include your email address, Firebase user identifier, authentication provider, account status, and related authentication records. When you use Google sign-in, Google and Firebase process the information needed to complete that sign-in.

Guest mode does not require you to create a FieldWorks account.

Facebook sign-in is not available in the current build.

Project content

Project names, descriptions, project types, stages, stage dates, reminders, comments, photos, videos, and voice or text notes you add are stored locally in the app on your device in the current release. The app does not currently synchronize this project content to a FieldWorks cloud account or make it available across your devices.

If you choose a file through Android’s file picker, including a file presented from a connected cloud storage app, FieldWorks receives access to the file you select so it can add it to your project. FieldWorks does not use a separate Google Drive connection to browse your Drive.

Project content may contain personal information about other people. Please only record or upload information that you have the right to use and share.

Device permissions and technical information

The app may use the camera to capture photos or videos and the microphone to record voice notes when you start those features. Selected or captured files and recordings are stored locally in the app. Android and Google Play may process device and app information under their own terms when you use Android system features or obtain the app through Google Play.

The current app configuration includes Firebase Authentication. We do not currently use FieldWorks analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs in the app. Google may also process service and technical information, such as app identifiers and IP addresses, when providing Firebase services, under its own terms.

Purchases

Subscription screens in the current development release are previews. The “Pay now” action is disabled, and FieldWorks does not currently collect card, e-wallet, or payment account details through those screens. If and when subscriptions launch, Google Play will process purchases and related transaction information under its own terms, and this policy will be updated before payment is enabled.

3. Why we use information

We use account information to provide account creation, sign-in, sign-out, and password reset; to protect the sign-in process; and to respond to account support requests.

We use project content and device permissions to provide the project-journaling features you request. Project content currently remains on your device and is not used by FieldWorks for advertising, profiling, or sale.

We may use information you send us to provide support, maintain app security, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

4. Legal grounds for EEA users

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the following grounds, as appropriate:

  • Contract: to provide the account and app features you request.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure and maintain the sign-in service and respond to support or misuse, where those interests do not override your rights.
  • Consent: for device permissions and any processing that applicable law requires to be based on consent. You can manage camera and microphone permissions in Android settings; withdrawing a permission may disable the related feature.
  • Legal obligation: where processing is needed to comply with applicable law.

The app’s project content is stored on your device in the current release. Firebase Authentication is operated from US data centers, so account authentication information may be processed in the United States. Google’s Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms describe the transfer mechanisms and safeguards that may apply, including standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where applicable. Google’s Firebase privacy information and subprocessor list provide further details.

5. When information is shared

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We share information only as needed with:

  • Google Firebase Authentication, to provide account sign-in and management.
  • Google and Android system services, when you choose Google sign-in, use Android file/camera/microphone features, or install the app through Google Play.
  • Authorities or other parties, where the law requires it or where necessary to protect rights, users, and the service.
  • A successor organization, if a business transfer occurs, subject to applicable privacy law.

Google and other service providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

6. Storage, retention, and deletion

Project content and media remain in the app’s local storage on your device until you delete them, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling or clearing storage can permanently remove local project records and media. FieldWorks cannot recover local-only content after it is removed.

Firebase Authentication retains account information under Google’s Firebase terms and configuration. The current app does not provide an in-app account deletion feature. To request deletion of a FieldWorks sign-in account, contact rizaljamin@gmail.com from the email address associated with the account. Account deletion does not erase project content stored locally on your device; delete that content in the app or clear app storage separately.

We will retain information we receive directly only as long as needed for the purpose collected, unless a longer period is required by law or needed to resolve a dispute.
